Section 1 of 5 — Where is AFL played?

Victoria is the heartland — but the game now spans the nation

Nine of eighteen AFL clubs are based in Victoria, the birthplace of the game. Over the past four decades the league deliberately expanded north and west — to Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Sydney, and the Gold Coast — to build a truly national competition. The map below shows how unevenly the clubs are distributed, and where the biggest stadiums sit. Victoria's dominance is unmistakable: it holds nearly half the clubs and the two largest venues on earth designed for Australian rules football.

Section 2 of 5 — Who has been winning?

Melbourne surged to the top — while some clubs are stuck in a cycle of losing

In 2019, Melbourne was a mid-table club sitting eleventh. By 2021 they were premiers — ending a 57-year drought — with an extraordinary 86% win rate. Geelong has been consistently elite for a decade, winning the 2022 flag. At the other end, North Melbourne have failed to win even a third of their games in any of the past five seasons. Section 3 of 5 — The crowd story

COVID emptied every stadium in 2020 — fans came roaring back by 2023

Over 6.9 million fans attended AFL matches in 2019. Then the pandemic hit. In 2020, that figure collapsed to just 826,000 — a drop of 88% — as state governments imposed crowd restrictions or moved games interstate into biosecure hubs. Restrictions eased slowly in 2021, and by 2023 the crowds had not just recovered but smashed the all-time record, reaching 7.47 million. The right chart shows how individual venues performed in 2023: the MCG and Perth Stadium led the country in fill rate, while the smaller Gold Coast and Sydney venues still struggled to attract full houses.

Section 4 of 5 — Who are the players?

Midfielders touch the ball most — but the best players are dominant in every facet

AFL positions shape what a player does on the field. Midfielders average 28+ disposals per game — significantly more than forwards or defenders — but elite forwards are the goal-kickers who win matches. Some defenders, such as Jack Sinclair and Luke Ryan, accumulate disposals at a rate rivalling midfielders thanks to their rebounding role. Section 5 of 5 — Who are the champions?

Hawthorn's dynasty is over — Geelong and Melbourne signal a new era of AFL power

Between 2013 and 2015, Hawthorn won three consecutive premierships — one of the great dynasties in Australian sport. Richmond then dominated 2017–2020 with three flags in four years. In 2021, Melbourne finally ended a 57-year drought to lift the cup. Geelong backed up with the flag in 2022 with an 81-point demolition of Sydney, before Collingwood claimed a tight four-point win over Brisbane in 2023.
